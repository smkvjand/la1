Why Plan A Wins for Culture

Plan A offers a more meaningful, slowed-down experience. The visit to the Sikh Gurdwara for Langar (community meal) provides a profound cultural lesson for children about hospitality and service. Finishing at the Getty for sunset is less rushed and highly cinematic.

star

Why Plan B Wins for Icons

If your goal is to "see it all," Plan B is optimized for proximity and traffic flow. By hitting the Getty in the morning, you avoid the late-afternoon rush and can spend more focused time in the DTLA arts district. Best for families with older teens or rapid sightseers.
